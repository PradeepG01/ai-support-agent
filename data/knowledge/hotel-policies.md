# Seaside Grand Hotel – Stay Policies

## Check-in and check-out
Check-in time is 2:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in from 10:00 AM is available on request, subject to room availability, at no extra charge. Late check-out until 2:00 PM costs INR 1,500.

## Cancellation policy
Bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the check-in date. Cancellations within 48 hours are charged one night's room rate. No-shows are charged the full stay.

## Identity documents
All guests must show a valid government photo ID at check-in, such as an Aadhaar card, passport or driving licence. Foreign nationals must present a passport and valid visa.

## Pets
Small pets under 10 kg are welcome in ground-floor rooms for a cleaning fee of INR 1,000 per stay. Pets are not allowed in the restaurant or pool area.

## Payments
We accept UPI, all major credit and debit cards, and cash. A refundable security deposit of INR 2,000 is collected at check-in and returned at check-out.
