# Seaside Grand Hotel – Amenities and Services

## Breakfast and dining
Breakfast is served in the Coral Restaurant from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM and is included with all Deluxe rooms. In-room dining is available 24 hours a day.

## Swimming pool and gym
The rooftop swimming pool is open from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM. The fitness centre on the second floor is open 24 hours with key-card access.

## Wi-Fi
Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel. Connect to the network "SeasideGuest" and log in with your room number and last name.

## Airport transfers
Airport pickup and drop can be booked through the front desk at least 12 hours in advance. A one-way sedan transfer costs INR 1,800.

## Parking
Free valet parking is available for hotel guests. Electric-vehicle charging points are in basement level B1.

## Laundry
Same-day laundry service is available if clothes are handed over before 10:00 AM.
